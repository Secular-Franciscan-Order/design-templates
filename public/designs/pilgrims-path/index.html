01 · A life in the world

Not apart from life.
Deeper within it.

Secular Franciscans are Catholic laypeople and diocesan clergy who follow Christ in the footsteps of Saint Francis—without leaving the homes, work, parishes, and communities entrusted to them.

We seek a life marked by prayer, fraternity, simplicity, service, and care for creation. The fraternity gives us companions for that journey: people who keep returning together from Gospel to life and from life to Gospel.

“Secular” names the place of the vocation: right here, in the world.

04 · A path of discernment

Walk slowly enough to listen.

Formation is a gradual process of prayer, learning, practice, and free discernment—not a fast track to a decision.

  1. 1

    About 3–6 months

    Orientation

    Visit the fraternity, ask questions, and begin to explore Franciscan spirituality and vocation.

  2. 2

    About 6 months

    Inquiry

    Learn more about the Franciscan family, the Church, and the shape of Secular Franciscan life.

  3. 3

    Generally 18 months–3 years

    Candidacy

    Deepen prayer, study the Rule, and discern a lasting commitment with the formation team.

  4. 4

    A lifelong commitment

    Profession

    Make a public promise to live the Gospel according to the Rule of the Secular Franciscan Order.

Formation unfolds gradually. These timeframes are approximate; the local formation team will confirm the process with each person.

Questions for the road

Wondering is welcome.

01Who are Secular Franciscans?

They are Catholic laypeople and diocesan clergy who live in the world and follow the Gospel in the spirit of Saint Francis. Members come from many walks of life and may be single or married.

02Would I have to leave my family, parish, or career?

No. Secular Franciscan life is lived within everyday responsibilities. Home, work, parish, and neighborhood become the places where the vocation takes shape.

03Is visiting a commitment?

Not at all. A first visit is simply a chance to meet the fraternity, experience the gathering, and ask honest questions.

04What happens during formation?

Formation combines prayer, conversation, study, and discernment. It moves through orientation, inquiry, and candidacy before a person decides whether to make profession.

05What does profession mean?

Profession is a public, lifelong promise to live the Gospel according to the Rule of the Secular Franciscan Order. It follows a substantial period of formation and free discernment.
